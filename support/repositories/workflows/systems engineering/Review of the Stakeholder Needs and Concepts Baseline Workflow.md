# Review of the Stakeholder Needs and Concepts Baseline Workflow

## Description

This workflow is destined to review and update the stakeholder needs and concepts baseline

It's need to ensure mistakes unsupervisioned in later activities don't pass to futher activities, ensure all stakeholders have a common understanding about the solution and all related parts was considered in the systems discussion and agreements.

For then, this workflow have the purpose to:

- Review the baseline documents for mistakes
- Review the baseline with stakeholders to define a common understand and agreement

The main work product is the:

- The revised documents
- The current documents as baseline.
