# System Statement Concept Workflow

## Description

This workflow is to develop the 'System Statement' section, describing what the system is and their role to satisfy the stakeholder needs.

It must be made following the 'Problem-Space' partitions defined in the [Problem Agreement](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md) document.

For then, this workflow have the purpose to:

- Describe the system mission, vision, purpose and goals.
- Refine related diagrams.
- Describe constraints and limitation about the system roles and functionality.

The main work product to be generated is:

- The [System Statement](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md#System%20Statement) section in the [Statement of Work](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md) document.

## Prerequisites

- The [Problem Agreement](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md) document is mature with the [Problem Partitions](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Problem%20Partitions) endorsed.

## Principles

- **No Single-Shot:** The 'System Statement' is an iterative work, do not expect a 'single-shot' approach to develop a mature version.

## Events

## Course of Actions

**Prepare:**

1. Fill the [Problem Definition](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md#Problem%20Definition) section with the [Problem Agreement](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md) informations:
   1. The abstract found in the [Symptoms and Consequences](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Symptoms%20and%20Consequences)
   2. The [Problem Diagnosis](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Problem%20Diagnosis)
   3. The [Problem Partitions](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Problem%20Partitions)
   4. A reference for the [Problem Agreement](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md) document.
2. Fill the [System Mission](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md#System%20Mission) section with the [Problem Partitions](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Problem%20Partitions).
3. Fill the goals in the [System Purpose and Goals](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md#System%20Purpose%20and%20Goals) section with the [Problem Partitions](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Problem%20Partitions).

**Elicit Stakeholder needs:**

- > [!iteration] Until the 'System Statement' acceptable describes what the system is and their role to satisfy their needs.
	1.  **Elicit System Problem:**
	      1. Fill the [Problem Definition](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md#Problem%20Definition) section with the [Problem Agreement](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md) information:
	         1. The abstract found in the [Symptoms and Consequences](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Symptoms%20and%20Consequences)
	         2. The [Problem Diagnosis](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Problem%20Diagnosis)
	         3. The [Problem Partitions](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Problem%20Partitions)
	         4. A reference for the [Problem Agreement](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md) document.
	      2. Fill the [System Mission](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md#System%20Mission) section with the [Problem Partitions](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Problem%20Partitions).
	      3. Fill the goals in the [System Purpose and Goals](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md#System%20Purpose%20and%20Goals) section with the [Problem Partitions](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Problem%20Partitions).
   2. **Elicit System Description:**
      - > [!note] You can use the [System Boundary Context](../../../../documents/engineering/System%20Boundary%20Context.md) and the storytelling approach to create the vision.
      - > [!constraint] The 'General Foundation' must describe the system high-level strategic role in the organisation.
      1. Derive the general '[System Mission](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md#System%20Mission)' from the [Problem Agreement](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md) describing the strategic system impact in the organisation.
      2. Derive the 'System Vision' from the 'System Mission'.
      3. Update the [System Boundary Context](../../../../documents/engineering/System%20Boundary%20Context.md) to reflect the 'System Vision'.
      4. Derive the general [System Purpose](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md#System%20Purpose%20and%20Goals) from the 'System Vision'
      5. Derive the general [System Goals](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md#System%20Purpose%20and%20Goals) from the 'System Purpose'
      6. If necessary refine both [System Boundary Context](../../../../documents/engineering/System%20Boundary%20Context.md) and the [As-Is Diagram]
   3. **Elicit System Limitations:**
      - > [!note] The development of this activity can lead to return to any previous activity.
      1. Limit the 'Problem Space' eliciting the external and internal constraints, and specific limitations, assumptions, and uncertainties according to the [Problem Partitions](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md#Problem%20Partitions) decomposition, that affect:
         1. The Solution
         2. The Project
   4. **Refine The Document:**
      1. Refine the 'System Statement' in the [Statement of Work](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md) :
         - > [!note] The development of this activity can lead to the need to return to previous activity.
         - > [!check] The 'System Vision' must describe an high view of the interactions with the users, the environment, operations and limitations.
         - > [!check] The 'System Mission' must describe the fundamental, enduring purpose of the system, focusing on its core strategic contribution and reason for existence.
         - > [!check] The 'System Purpose' must describe an high view course of action to satisfy the 'System Mission'.
         - > [!check] The 'System Goals' must describe an high view steps to satisfy the 'System Purpose'
         - > [!check] The 'System Vision' must be directly traceable to and clearly demonstrate how the system will fulfill the 'System Mission'.
         - > [!check] The 'System Vision' must be structured to encompass the course of action defined by the 'System Purpose' and the key achievements outlined in the 'System Goals'.
      2. Update the [Statement of Work](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Statement%20of%20Work.md) attaching:
         1. The [System Boundary Context](../../../../documents/engineering/System%20Boundary%20Context.md)
         2. The [Problem Agreement](../../../../documents/enterprise/agreements/Problem%20Agreement.md)
