# Needs Catalog

This catalog contains the needs elicited from the [Stakeholder Catalog](Stakeholder%20Catalog.md), the compilation and their justification is coded  in the document [Needs Catalog Spreedsheet](Needs%20Catalog%20Spreedsheet.ods) classified as follow:

- **Must Have:** Innegotiable needs that must be satisfied in current iteration.
- **Should have:** Needs that is desired and should be considered in next iteration.
- **Could have:** Needs that could be discussed for reanalysis in further iteration, but without prevision to be satisfied.
- **Won't Have:** Needs that was not considered at moment, it could be re evaluated in further iterations but currently is not scheduled as a priority.
