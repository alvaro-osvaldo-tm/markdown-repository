# Enterprise View

## Visualisation Levels

- **Level 1 - Landscape:** Provides and a High-level view of the enterprise expected outcome and their drivers and stakeholders.
- **Level 2 - Capabilities:** Provides a High-level view of the enduring abilities to achieve the enterprise outcome. Is independent from systems implementation and provides an abstraction for their functions.
- **Level 3 - Business:** Related to processes, roles, goals, the interaction with customers and providers, and other organisational structures
- **Level 4 - Operations:** Describe how the enterprise do their resource transformation and their related results.
